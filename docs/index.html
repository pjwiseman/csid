CSID Phase 2 (Australia)

Reference lists for sucrase-isomaltase deficiency using Australian composition (English / AUSNUT 2023; sucrose from AFCD). Foods with ≤1 g sucrose and ≤1 g starch per 100 g are treated as allow. Higher-sucrose foods stay on the avoid list unless you are testing a defined portion. Passionfruit is allow at English moderate (1.4 g sucrose / 100 g).
