Bad foods

Foods above the Australian ≤1 g/100 g low band for sucrose and/or starch. Some have a calculated ~1 g sucrose portion for Phase 2 testing — see notes.

Fats

  • Butter
  • Margarine with whey or milk solids

Milk and milk products

  • Flavoured yoghurt
  • Milk and milk products (general)

Fruits

  • Apple
  • Apricot
  • Banana
  • Date
  • Grapefruit
  • Honeydew (white skin)
  • Mandarin
  • Mango
  • Melon
  • Orange
  • Peach
  • Pineapple
  • Rockmelon
  • Watermelon

Vegetables

  • Beetroot
  • Brown onion
  • Carrot
  • Corn
  • Green peas
  • Legumes (black beans, chickpeas, lentils, etc.)
  • Okra
  • Potato (all types)
  • Pumpkin
  • Red onion
  • Squash (winter and starchy types)

Grains

  • Amaranth
  • Barley
  • Buckwheat
  • Bulgur
  • Corn (grain/flour)
  • Couscous
  • Grain flours (general)
  • Millet
  • Oats
  • Quinoa
  • Rice
  • Rolled oats
  • Rye
  • Spelt

  • Wheat
  • Wheat flour (thickener)
  • White rice

Meat and meat substitutes

  • Breaded meat
  • Canned corned beef
  • Cured meat
  • Edamame
  • Egg with added milk, flour, or sugar
  • Processed meat
  • Smoked meat

Nuts and seeds

Sugars and sweeteners

  • Lactose (milk sugar)
  • Maltose (grain sugar)
  • Sucrose (table sugar)
  • Syrups

Other

  • Worcestershire sauce
