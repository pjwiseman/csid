# All foods (CSID Phase 2 — Australian SSRD)

Single-page reference for every food in [data/foods.yaml](../data/foods.yaml). Use **Find** (Ctrl+F / Cmd+F) to search by name, category, or status.

Individual tolerance varies — see [good-foods.md](./good-foods.md) and [bad-foods.md](./bad-foods.md) for grouped lists. Phase 1 → Phase 2 category changes: [phase-changes.md](./phase-changes.md). On the website, Phase 1/Phase 2 columns on [all foods](../docs/all-foods.html) are hidden until you tick **Show Phase 1 vs Phase 2**.

Source: [English — SID in Australia](https://www.drcarolynenglish.com/post/what-can-i-eat-with-sucrase-isomaltase-deficiency-in-australia) (AUSNUT 2023 / AFCD). Step 1 baseline: [Ideal Nutrition — CSID management guide](https://www.idealnutrition.com.au/csid-how-to-manage-sucrase-isomaltase-deficiency/)
