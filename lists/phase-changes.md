# Phase 1 → Phase 2 category changes

**Phase 1** is the Ideal Nutrition disaccharide-free elimination list (including foods added before the Australian reassess). **Phase 2** is the current Australian SSRD list ([English](https://www.drcarolynenglish.com/post/what-can-i-eat-with-sucrase-isomaltase-deficiency-in-australia) / AUSNUT / AFCD).

Lookup table: [data/phase1.yaml](../data/phase1.yaml). Website: [changes.html](../docs/changes.html). Optional Phase 1/Phase 2 columns: [all-foods.html](../docs/all-foods.html).

## Reclassified

Foods that existed in Phase 1 and now have a different good/bad status.

| Food | Phase 1 | Phase 2 | Category | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Honeydew (yellow skin) | Bad | Good | Fruits | Golden/yellow-skin honeydew. English Table 1 (≤1 g sucrose and starch / 100 g). AFCD R3 peeled raw 1.0 g sucrose, 0 g starch, 0 g maltose. White-skin honeydew is 3.9 g sucrose — listed separately as avoid. USDA ~2.5 g/100 g is a US blend, not this cultivar. |
| Papaya | Bad | Good | Fruits | Pawpaw/papaya. English Table 1. AFCD R3 peeled raw 0 g sucrose, 0 g starch (red and yellow papaya also 0 g sucrose). Ideal Nutrition Step 1 listed papaya as avoid (US lists). |
| Pear | Bad | Good | Fruits | Green-skin, Packham, William Bartlett, nashi, and canned-in-juice. English Table 1. AFCD R3 sucrose 0.5–0.8 g/100 g, starch ≤0.2 g. Brown-skin pear is 2.4 g sucrose — treat as higher, not this allow entry. Ideal Nutrition Step 1 listed pear as avoid. |
| Raspberry | Bad | Good | Fruits | Fresh or unsweetened frozen. English Table 1. AFCD R3 raw 0.5 g sucrose, 0.2 g starch. Canned in syrup is higher sucrose — avoid. Ideal Nutrition Step 1 listed raspberry as avoid. |
| Strawberry | Bad | Good | Fruits | Fresh or unsweetened frozen. English Table 1. AFCD R3 raw 0 g sucrose, 0.1 g starch. Canned in syrup is higher sucrose — avoid. Ideal Nutrition Step 1 listed strawberry as avoid. |

**5** reclassified.
